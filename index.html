02 / THE STORYNOT STARTING OVER. STARTING WITH PURPOSE.

I never stopped being curious.
I never forgot the sky.

Several years ago, I received an invitation to an Emirates recruitment event. I did not take that path then. Yet the idea of flying stayed with me.

During the pandemic, I moved to Lisbon and worked at Majorel on a Google/YouTube project. I later spent more than three years at Minderest, supporting international customers in the world of data and pricing. At Bodytone, I led Customer Success while developing data systems and smarter ways to serve people.

Along the way, I learned programming, analytics, automation and AI. Those skills changed what I can build. My experiences with people changed how I listen.

“Technology is what I learned to build. Working with people is what I want to do.”

I am not returning to aviation because I ran out of alternatives. I am choosing it because I know the difference I want to make: calm, considered service, attentive teamwork and respect for safety.

08 / WHAT CONNECTS MY WORLDSONE JOURNEY, DIFFERENT DISCIPLINES

MY METHOD, IN EVERY ROLE

From people to systems.
Back to people.

I did not learn to program because I stopped caring about people. I wanted to solve the problems I had seen first-hand: confusing information, avoidable frustration, and systems that did not help the person in front of me.

Those skills became a new way to support teams. Returning to aviation is another deliberate choice, bringing the same curiosity and attention to a profession where safety, teamwork and human connection matter every day.

01Listen

Understand the person before the process.

02Assess

Keep calm, clarify needs and respect procedures.

03Act

Coordinate a useful response with the team.

04Follow through

Build trust through consistency, not promises.

One person. Several disciplines. My professional work, software experiments and yoga teaching explore different aspects of the same principle: pay attention, communicate clearly and keep learning.

09 / REAL EXPERIENCEA SITUATION THAT SHAPED ME

Trust isn't built
when everything goes right.

A VIP client at Minderest was upset by an increase in fees. Rather than becoming defensive, I made myself more available, increased our meetings, visited the team and built relationships with senior management and investors.

I used what I was learning in data analysis to help them make better use of the service, and became a trusted point of contact during difficult moments.

That experience reinforced my approach to service: listen, take responsibility, stay present and follow through.
